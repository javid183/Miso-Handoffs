## As built

- **Kill rule:** `KillFeedbackRules.IsKill(hasUnit, unitFaction, playerFaction) => hasUnit && unitFaction != playerFaction`. The presenter passes `unit.Faction` and `CombatLevel.Instance.PlayerFaction` and does nothing when there is no `CombatLevel`. So Bergsons and Toba (player faction) get no flash, shake or sting. Enemy, neutral and enemy-Toba deaths do, including `FakeDie`, which also raises `unitDied`.
- **Flash:** the material comes from `unit.GetMainVisual()`, and the flash runs only when its `sharedMaterial` has `_ColorOverride`. The presenter reads the default from `sharedMaterial`, then `DOTween.Kill(material)` (the blink's `OnKill` restores the default). It sets `_ColorOverride` to white and runs `DOColor(default, 0.1 s)` with a 0.1 s delay, linear, scaled time. The tween's `OnKill` restores the default, it is linked to the visual's GameObject, and the presenter kills its live tweens in `OnDestroy`. `GUnit` is unchanged.
  - `DOTween.Kill(material)` should stop the blink: DOTween's `Material.DOColor(..., property, ...)` shortcut sets the material as the tween target, and `PlayHitBlink` uses that shortcut on `bodyVisual.material`, the same instance `GetMainVisual().material` returns. **Not confirmed in play.**
- **Chain:** `NextChainStep(prev, chainEnd, now, maxStep)` returns 0 when `now >= chainEnd`, otherwise `clamp(prev + 1, 0, maxStep)`, with `maxStep = chainShakes.Length - 1` (2). On each kill `chainEnd = Time.time + shake.Duration` (0.25 s, scaled), so the chain ends 0.25 s after the last kill shake started. Kills 1 to 4 in one chain give 0, 1, 2, 2. The shake is played with `shake.Apply(CameraHandler.Instance)`, the body of `FXRunner.PlayCFX`.
- **Sting:** `AudioHandler.Instance.PlaySFX(stingSfxId)` when the step is 0 (the first kill of a chain), so once per chain. Placeholder id **`A_SFX_John_ShieldImpact`** (an existing, non-looping, short impact). It is a serialized field, so it can be swapped in the Inspector.

## Decisions I made

- **Bus subscription follows the bus (Tier 1, same behaviour).** The plan says to subscribe in `OnEnable` and unsubscribe in `OnDisable`. But `GlobalEventBus` lives on `LevelBase.prefab`, which is spawned after the container scene loads, so in `OnEnable` `Instance` is usually null or a stale bus. The presenter does subscribe in `OnEnable`, unsubscribe in `OnDisable`, and re-binds in `Update` when `GlobalEventBus.Instance` changes. This is the pattern `RoleZonePresenter` already uses for the same reason. Without it the presenter would never hear a death in the real flow.
- The shake is played with `shake.Apply(CameraHandler.Instance)` directly instead of through an `FXRunner` reference. `FXRunner.PlayCFX` is exactly that call, and this keeps the scene object to one component.
- The chain end uses the played asset's `Duration` (0.25 s) rather than a separate serialized window, so the chain always matches the shake.
- The object sits under `---Management---`, next to `StatsHoverHandler`, rather than at the scene root (same size of diff, tidier hierarchy).
- `TestLevel.unity` got the object too. It is a standalone combat scene: it has the bus, `CombatLevel`, the camera and the audio. It is older than the container scene, though, and lacks `ShowStatsHoverPreview`.

## Risks and loose ends

- `DOTween.Kill(material)` stopping the blink is reasoned from the DOTween shortcut, not seen in play. If a blink keeps running over the flash, it needs a `GUnit` change, which this chunk does not make.
- A hit that blinks a unit after its death (a later hit of a multi-hit ability on the same corpse) would start a new blink tween while the fade runs; both would write `_ColorOverride`. It is unlikely, and it ends at the default either way.
- `Die(delay)` deactivates the unit's GameObject after its delay. If that delay is under 0.2 s, the flash is cut short visually. Harmless.
- A sequence or ability shake starting within 0.25 s of a kill is skipped (priority 0 under 1), as the plan's Risks already say.
- Hit slow-mo (time scale above 0) stretches the scaled flash and shake, which the design accepts.
- `FakeDie` on an enemy-faction unit (the Dom boss fight's early end) gets the full kill beat. That matches the rule. Flag it if it reads wrong in that cutscene.

## How to see it

Play a mission from the normal flow (the container scene). The object is `---Management--- / KillFeedback` in `CombatLevelContainerScene`.

1. **Single kill:** finish an enemy with a basic attack. The sprite flashes white (about 0.1 s hold, quick fade), the camera kicks harder than a normal hit, and the placeholder sting plays (John's shield impact).
2. **Area kill of 2 or 3:** kill 2 or 3 enemies with one area ability (Lucy's Cyclone, or Linda's Explosive Arrow on a cluster). Each victim flashes, the kick grows with each kill (0.05, 0.06, 0.07), and the sting plays once.
3. **Kill during another shake:** kill an enemy with one of Linda's shots, or while the Masher's attack shake runs. The kill kick plays in full and isn't cut by the hit or ability shake that follows. With no kill, those shakes look as before.
4. **Family death, no beat:** let a Bergson or Toba (ally) die, or use the cheat handler. No white flash, no extra kick, no sting.
5. **Mark's or a role kill:** let Mark or a Family Role QTE finish an enemy. Same beat as step 1.
6. In the Inspector on `KillFeedback`, change the hold and fade times, the shake list or the sting id to try other values.
