## Decisions I made

- **API deviation, needs the coordinator's OK (Tier 3 shape, additive):** the planned `GUnit.ShowDamagePreview(float total, float confirmed)` can't carry the D-135 rule. A non-final pick and the firing pick have the same (total, confirmed) values but need different skulls (none vs tinted), and a single-target preview with confirmed = 0 must keep today's one-colour look. So the overload has a third optional parameter: `ShowDamagePreview(float total, float confirmed, DamagePreviewPick pick = DamagePreviewPick.Single)`. It can still be called as `(total, confirmed)`, and the old method calls it with confirmed = 0 as briefed. The new public enums live in a non-core UI file (`DamagePreviewRules.cs`). If this shape is unwanted, the alternative is a second `bool firingClick` parameter, which can't do the "Picking with nothing confirmed" light tint.
- Segment order: from the left, the remaining health, then this click's share, then the confirmed share up to the current HP. This is drain order: the confirmed shots take the HP first.
- While picking, the light tint also covers a target with nothing confirmed yet (the first hover of a volley draws all its damage light). It turns to the normal decrease tint once picked. "Picking" means `MultipleTargets && RequiredTargetCount > 1`; anything else keeps the old single-segment path.
- Normal skull: the decrease segment turns deep red and the skull is untinted. "This click" skull: the skull takes the light tint and the segments keep their colours (only the confirmed segment turns deep red, and only when the confirmed shots alone kill).
- The lethal check in the panel uses `DamagePreviewRules.IsLethal`, which matches `PredictsLethalOn` (`damage >= hp`; the preview only stores damage > 0). I didn't edit `AbilityDamagePreview` to share the function (it's core and the brief said not to touch it), so the "pinned together" test pins the rule's cases rather than calling `PredictsLethalOn`.
- Pulse in its own small component on unscaled time (the feedback-pass design says UI pulses run on unscaled time).
- Undo of a pick: the confirmed segments are redrawn in `TargetSelectionState.OnEnterState`. The hovered part comes back on the next mouse move, not right after the right-click (the hover event hasn't fired again).

## How to see it

1. Open a level with Lucy (any M1/M2 combat level), with Lucy's Primary Attack at 2+ orbs.
2. Select Lucy and arm the Primary Attack. Hover an enemy: its bar shows one light-orange segment (this click). If that shot alone would kill and Lucy has more than one orb left to pick, there is **no** skull.
3. Click the enemy once. Hover it again: the first shot is now the normal red segment and the second is light orange beside it. Move the cursor to an empty tile or off range: the enemy keeps the red (confirmed) segment.
4. Keep picking the same low-HP enemy until the confirmed shots alone kill: the skull appears at the bar's left, pulsing, and the red segment goes deep red.
5. On the last orb, hover an enemy that confirmed + this click would kill, but confirmed alone wouldn't: the skull shows tinted orange.
6. Right-click once: the last pick is removed and the bars redraw without it. Right-click again until back at unit selection: all previews clear. Pick all targets: on fire, all clear.
7. Single-target check: John's attack on an enemy he can kill shows the skull with a deep-red segment; on one he can't kill, the bar looks as before.
8. Turn the option off: `Resources/GlobalSettings` › Visuals › Display Lethal Mark Preview. The skull and the deep-red tint no longer show.
