# Creative critique: onboarding after the Hearth (option B)

2026-11-10. Read-only critic (Explore agent following `.claude/agents/miso-design-critic.md`, lens creative; the critic agent type wasn't available in the session). Saved by the coordinator, content unchanged.

## 1. Strongest objection

The plan wants The Pile's last turn to be the sisters' first real Duo killing the west lane, with the tally going up for both, and then "Still counting?" / "...No." over it. That merges two different beats of the S8 arc (first-hour design, "The Sister's Rivalry arc") into one, and it gets the feeling backwards.

- **The Turn is meant to be the joke dying under dread.** The first-hour design says: "The barks stop; they fight back to back. The counter shows alone... then fades." "We both did" is saved for the M4 payoff, after the Yard, where they "find racing works better when they set each other up."
- **What the plan stages instead is a triumph.** A mash QTE and a shared multi-kill, the HUD counter going up for both just before the line that should kill it. A cold player hears "Still counting?" as a reaction to a score ("nice, we tied"), not to a pile of dead animals that might hold Ryker.
- **The rest of the arc is emptied out.** The Yard scene's discovery (setting each other up) has already happened on the battlefield, so Home visit 3 (THJ: "the nicest part of the entire onboarding") is left with nothing to discover. M4's "We both did" becomes a repeat.
- **The plan contradicts itself.** The Trail's table lists Marks-Sisters up column 4 as a ★ answer for the ledge Archers. A player who follows that ★ fires the first real Duo at The Trail, unframed: breaks acceptance criterion 4 and S8 rule 4 ("every first gets a story beat").
- **Cold run, either way it fails:** the Duo goes at The Trail, then The Pile ends on a stray bow shot under "Still counting?"; or the Duo lands at The Pile and reads as a victory lap. No hint by design, so the moment depends on one button out of a full bar.
- **What I'd do:** take Marks-Sisters out of The Trail's ★ list, and don't make the Duo The Pile's finisher. Let The Pile end on the sisters fighting apart but back to back, counter alone. Keep the Duo for M4 or after the Yard.
- **Bears on:** no THJ question covers this; add one (next to Q1).

## 2. Other risks

- **The searches lose the search; "no real story" still not fixed.**
  - "A clue line on every beat" is promised, but the River's stage change plays R7-7 to R7-9 (the Shield Flare lines, not clues). The only clue left on the grid is "note 16,3 clue" at the end.
  - Clue lines R7-2 to R7-5 have no placement (prints, blood, white fur, Claws "cut down with a stone axe"). R7-5 sets up Toba and needs Claws someone else killed, which an awake rush replaces. On the Ridge only G9-1 is placed; G9-2 to G9-4 (herbs, wrapper) aren't.
  - Cold playtest: at the Map Table, V2-3 and V2-6 recite clues the player never saw.
  - Fix: one clue tile or close-up per beat, written into the grids, not left to narrative later. Better as its own question.
- **The Synergy "lesson" brings back the tutorial feel THJ wants gone after the Hearth.**
  - River beat 2: camera pan, "Lucy. If it gets hot, aim at me." before anything is hot, then a gold goal tile "John: hold the ford." = a tutorial step dressed as dialogue. "*At* you?" / "Trust me." only lands as trust if the player chooses to ember Dad.
  - Cold playtest: follows the tile, embers John because told to, "OK, done", no surprise.
  - Fix: keep the islands on the diagonals and the line (triggered by John's first Shield Up near the ford); make the objective "Silence the archers", not a tile for John. Ridge similar, milder: "From the far side." gives away the solution.
  - Bears on: Q6 and the "No panels" rule.
- **The Pile's search turns into an ambush trigger, the genre default.** Step next to the pile, enemies pop out; the search answers nothing; relief only in T11-7 rounds later. A one-move search with a gold tile is a checkbox, not the tempo drop THJ asked for. Fix: the search gives a partial answer first (Lucy: "No white fur..."), then the ambush, so they fight with hope. Bears on Q1.
- **Memory 4 ends with enemies on the board, and "John cuts the line" isn't felt.** THJ: "the first Duo should finish everyone"; option (a) leaves left-side Claws standing. Switching off the pools is data only, no visual, no line. The fade-out step now does cheaply what Q4 (b)/(c) priced: Mark "...and the rest ran", leftovers fade; optionally John fades as the telling hands over to the Duo. Add one visible pool-collapse beat with a John line at (6,5). Recommend (a) + fade-outs, no extra turn.
- **No quiet stretch.** Every post-Hearth level is rush, lesson, rush in 3–5 rounds; the route choice (Lucy's "Her blood.", the one place the player voices worry by choosing) is cut. Warmth lives in barks and Home visit 1. The V1 handover is the plan's best creative addition: keep it, with a real clue line. Recommend Q5(a).

## 3. Per option

- **A:** don't ship; corridors with no moment.
- **B:** ship with changes: no Duo at The Trail or as The Pile's finisher; clue tiles/close-ups in every beat; Synergy lessons led by layout and a line, not a goal tile; the Pile's search gives a partial answer before the ambush; Memory 4 leftovers cleared with a fade-out plus a visible line-cut.
- **C:** don't ship; loses "two go, two stay" and visit 1's reason to exist.
- **D:** don't ship.

## 4. What would change my mind

- Duo: cold players who end The Pile with the Duo describe it as sad or as the joke ending, not a win, and the Yard scene still lands for them.
- Goal tile: cold players without it never ember John at the ford across two attempts.
- Clues: cold players at the Map Table can say "someone saved and is tending her" from the River and Ridge as built under B, without clue tiles.
