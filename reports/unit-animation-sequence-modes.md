## Which SetDirection, APTransfer, facing

- **`GUnit.SetDirection`**, not the handler's: it calls the handler's `SetDirection` and also updates the unit's cached facing, last non-up facing and spawn-source offset, so the unit and its animator don't disagree afterwards. It also zeroes x when y is non-zero.
- **APTransfer directions** (`AC_Playable_John.controller`): a 1D blend tree on `DirectionY`: -1 Down, 0 Side, 1 Up (clips `John-APTransfer-Down/Side/Up`). Side is flipped for left and right (John's default side mode applies). Only these three. All three clips have **Loop Time on**.
- **Facing chosen: (0,1), Up.** The target is up-left from (4,2) to (3,4): 2 up, 1 left. Up is about 26 degrees off, Left about 64. **Up-left cannot be shown**: no diagonal sprites exist, and `GUnit.SetDirection` drops x for any vertical component anyway. If THJ would rather see John point left, set `facing` to (-1,0).
- Facing isn't reset by Play (the handler keeps `_currentDirection` and the animator floats) or by Stop.

## Decisions I made

- John's asset uses `Loop` instead of the planned `HoldLastFrame`: his APTransfer clips are Loop Time, so the handler cannot freeze them and `HoldLastFrame` would also loop, with a warning on each play. THJ decided to keep Loop: the gesture repeats (about every 0.64 s) until BackToIdle.
- Rejected a Pause/Resume trick to fake a freeze: `Pause` leaves the handler's paused flag set, so a later `Play` would not restore the animator speed and the unit would stay frozen.
- `HoldLastFrame` on a looping clip logs a warning (mirror of the Loop warning).
- `Stop` applies facing after the idle returns; other modes before the state.
- Zero facing `(0,0)` with `setFacing` on logs a warning and is ignored.
- Added public static helpers `ModeWaitsForClip`, `ModeNeedsAnimationName`, `FindLivingUnitByNickname` for the tests.
