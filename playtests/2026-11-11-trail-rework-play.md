## Why there's no challenge

1. **The first volley decides every fight.** Enemies start lined up inside Linda's reach; at 10 HP (factor 0.6 rounds up) a pierce (6 then 4 with falloff) plus embers or an Explosive (8 on three) kills or cripples all of them before they act. Enemies hit the family 0–3 times per level.
2. **Each level is built around one ★ answer that clears it.** The answer is visible (and the aim preview now shows it), so finding it takes no thought, and nothing remains after it.
3. **Free tools stay open.** FTP starts full; the Mark–Lucy Duo (only Linda–Lucy is locked) wiped three Claws in one action, twice. Family Roles finish the crippled enemies in the enemy phase for nothing (Watch 3 kills, Lucy's role 2).
4. **Enemies barely threaten.** Claw 4 and Archer 3 damage against 15–32 HP; no enemy acts before the player, none starts in range of the family, none punishes a clump or a line.
5. **The layouts reuse the old maps.** The real change was HP, and HP went down.
