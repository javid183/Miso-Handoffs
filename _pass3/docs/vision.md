# Vision

## The game

**Children of Morta: Tactics** (codename Miso) reimagines the Bergson family's struggle against the Corruption as a grid-based, turn-based tactical RPG. It emphasizes cooperation and family-based combos while keeping the emotional weight, painterly pixel art and family-driven narrative of Children of Morta.

It is positioned as a Children of Morta IP collaboration, which needs licensing alignment with **Dead Mage** and **11 bit studios**.

## Pillars

1. **Grid-based tactical combat with reaction timing.** QTEs give the player agency throughout combat, on their own turn and the enemy's.
2. **Family-driven cooperative combat.** Duo and trio abilities, family synergies, family roles: the family fights as a family.
3. **Emotional narrative.** A family under pressure; warmth against dread.
4. **Elevated Children of Morta-style pixel art.**

## USPs

Every design is checked against these:

- **Family Roles** (family passives): each Bergson has one reactive role that protects or supports the family.
- **Synergies** (family combos): automatic, conditional effects between family members.
- **Duos**: deliberate paired abilities between two family members.
- **Managing the Home as a Family**: between missions, the family lives, works and grows together at Home.

## Inspirations

Children of Morta, XCOM, Marvel's Midnight Suns, Fire Emblem, Final Fantasy Tactics, Into the Breach, Persona 5.

## What playtests have liked so far

- QTEs: playtests found them fun everywhere, not only during the enemy phase.
- Passive synergies between units (e.g. Linda shooting into Lucy's cyclone; Sister's Rivalry, though it played as overpowered).

## Main loop

Pick missions on a world map. The story sets a main dungeon (a multi-level mission done in one run) with a deadline; smaller side missions are optional preparation. Between missions, the family returns Home.
