The Corruption's forces are goblins, beasts and two bosses. Each type asks for a different answer: hitting a Black Claw turns its pack on you, poking the Masher draws a counter-attack, and every Worm you kill splits into smaller ones. All enemies use the same turn rules as the family ([Combat rules](combat-rules.md)); difficulty per level comes from tiers (CR-32).

## Special rules

- **EN-1. Pact Leader (Black Claw).** When a Black Claw is attacked, every Dark Claw within 5 cells of it targets the attacker. Black Claws also share grudges with each other within 10 cells. The call also wakes the units it reaches (EN-15). *Built*, intended: within 5 cells, not all Dark Claws (D-193)
- **EN-2. Counter-attack (Masher Goblin).** When the Masher survives a hit, it strikes back at the attacker, walking into range first if it has to. It counters every hit, with no limit per turn, and its slam hits both sides. Hovering the Masher while targeting it shows the counter in advance: its path, the cells it will hit and the damage. An Unaware Masher (EN-12) is woken by the hit before the counter is decided, so it counters as usual, but its counter preview is hidden while it is Unaware (Q9). *Built*. No limit per turn is intended (D-098). No hit from Lucy's Cyclone provokes it: the Whirls, a Steer's hits on the way and its knock, the cast's knock and the burst (D-537, D-551, [Lucy](characters/lucy.md) LU-5); a Steer passing through it hits it without bursting the Cyclone. As a heavy, it walks through the Cyclone, its counter walk included, and bursts it on entering its cell (one hit on it and on each enemy beside the Cyclone, then the Cyclone is gone); the burst doesn't stop or turn the counter, which walks on and slams if its target is in reach (D-550, LU-7). Its counter step may end on the Cyclone's cell (D-571). *Built* **Plow (D-573, 2026-11-18):** the counter step also shoves the units standing in the cells it enters, enemy or player but not the attacker, one cell onward in the step direction, then steps; no damage. A step is planned only when every unit in the way can be pushed (single-cell, not Immovable, a free cell behind it) and the walk would take it; otherwise it takes another step or none. Pushed units start no Family Role when the counter-provokes-roles toggle is off; with it on, a shoved enemy landing in Linda's lane can still draw her lane shot (known gap, THJ declined the fix). The Masher's own-turn walk is unchanged. *Built* (play check pending)
- **EN-3. Immovable (Masher Goblin, Mountain Deev).** Can't be pushed, swapped or moved by Mark's Command. The Masher is also Reckless: it turns on whoever attacks it. *Built*. Nor pulled or knocked by Lucy's Cyclone, which also never pulls or knocks units larger than one cell (LU-3, LU-4, LU-6): a Steer passes through it and hits it, but never stops on it, and the cast is never aimed at it. Immovable or larger than one cell is what makes an enemy a **heavy** for the Cyclone (no new tag): heavies walk through it and burst it (LU-7, EN-2, EN-6)
- **EN-4. Split (Dark Worm).** The placed Worm and its first split pop on any hit, whatever the damage (1 HP in effect), and split into smaller worms. Only the final spawns have real HP: 4 HP, 2 damage, with max HP equal to that. *Planned* (D-192). As built: when a Worm dies it splits into 12 HP (6 damage), then 6 HP (4 damage), then 4 HP (2 damage) worms; children spawn with max HP 28 and odd current HP (one at 0). *Built, differs*
- **EN-5. Summon (Dark Shaman).** The Shaman calls 1 Dark Claw onto a random empty cell within 2 of itself (1 AP, cooldown 3). There is no cap, and summoned Claws stay when the Shaman dies. *Built*
- **EN-6. Goblin Queen.** A boss with a strike, a jump and a telegraphed charge. Her strike deals 7.5 × damage to each family member within 1 cell of the struck cell. She jumps (no damage) to one of four charge spots, marks the longest straight lane from there (wider than one cell; cooldown 4), and on her next turn charges to its far end: every family member next to a cell she passes takes 10 × damage and is pushed 1 cell. She has no phases. As a heavy (1 × 2), her walks and her charge pass through Lucy's Cyclone and may end on its cell (D-571), bursting it on entering, once even when several charge steps cover it: one hit on her and on each enemy beside the Cyclone, then it's gone and the charge goes on (D-550, [Lucy](characters/lucy.md) LU-7). Casting the Cyclone into her charge lane trades it for that hit; it doesn't stop the charge. Her jump never lands on it or bursts it (D-557, D-572). In M4, her death triggers the family's Family Super Time ([Missions and structure](missions-structure.md) MI-23). She is fought again in M10 with 150 HP. *Built.* **On `thj/home-test`:** under the Hungry Moon Omen, she gains +1 AP each turn while below 50% HP (D-011).
- **EN-7. Mountain Deev.** A static boss. In its level (M12) it has three actions: a wind-up that marks 10 random cells; poison pools dropped on those cells on its next action, which deal 6 × damage to a family member on the cell each tick and last 3 turns; and, if it was hit since its last turn, a wave that pushes every family member within 9 cells 2 cells away (no damage). The level removes its Summon, which would spawn 3 Deevoted and make the Deev take no damage until any other enemy dies. So in M12 the Deev can be hit from the first turn. The design says its Deevoted must be destroyed before it can be hit. *Built, differs*. **Planned (D-194):** restore the Summon in M12; the Deev takes no damage until all 3 Deevoted are destroyed (not when any other enemy dies)
- **EN-8. Deevoted.** The Deev's tentacles: no abilities, no rewards, ignored by objectives. They would die at 0 HP (about 1) or when their Inactive status is removed, which nothing does. Only the removed Summon spawns them, so they never appear on `main`. *Built* (unused). **Planned (D-194):** they die normally at 0 HP, and the Summon brings them back on `main`
- **EN-11. Dark Mending (Dark Shaman).** The Shaman heals one wounded dark ally within 3 cells (Manhattan distance, no line of sight needed) for 10 × its damage stat, never above the ally's max HP. It costs 1 AP, like the Summon, and does not end the Shaman's turn; cooldown 2. Dark allies are the units with the hidden `Kin.Dark` tag: Dark Claw, Black Claw, Dark Worm (split children too) and other Dark Shamans; never the Shaman itself. Stunned and dormant dark allies can be healed too (D-151). The heal is classed as an Ability, not an attack, so it is not part of the Shift danger zone or the threat arcs, which count Attack abilities only (CR-39, CR-43; read in code, not checked in play). The AI considers it only for an ally at **75%** of max HP or less and scores it 3.5 + 2 × the ally's missing HP fraction, + 0.5 on a Leader (the Black Claw): that beats summoning and normal attacks, but a shot that kills a family member still wins. The Shaman doesn't walk toward a wounded ally to heal it. It plays the `Heal` animation (a copy of the Summon's `SpawnEnemy`); the healed unit shows a green "+N" (CR-53), and the enemy's next-targets list leaves the healed ally out (it lists hostile units only). Known gap: hovering a Shaman that is about to heal still highlights the healed ally's cell. *Built* (D-150, D-151; on `main` since the 2026-10-23 fast-forward, `f4e9d9158`). The new graph and ability also change the AI regression test's output; that test stays known red and was not re-baselined. The design had it end the Shaman's turn; THJ chose 1 AP, with "ends the turn" as the fallback if it plays too strong (D-150).

## How enemies think

- **EN-9.** Enemies are run by a utility AI. Each unit scores its options (attack, move, use an ability) against a plan for the whole faction, then acts. A Taunted unit must go after its taunter. An Unaware enemy doesn't act at all ([Alert state](#alert-state-unaware-and-alerted), EN-12). *Built*
- **EN-18. Dangerous ground.** The AI has a rule for ground that hurts an enemy at the end of its turn: it doesn't end a move there unless an attack it still has this turn (AP and cooldown counted) reaches a target from there; walking across is fine, an enemy already standing there isn't made to leave, and charges and dash attacks aren't filtered; the intent preview uses the same rule. **No ground in the game is dangerous today:** Lucy's Cyclone cross was the only source and stopped being one, because enemies kept off it and the Cyclone felt useless (D-588, reversing D-584). Enemies now walk onto its arms and end moves there freely, so the Whirl pulls and hits them ([Lucy](characters/lucy.md) LU-4, LU-7). The rule and its core hooks stay, with no source; round 2's heavy exception to it and the heavy-warning wobble are gone with it (D-584). Known gap: the next-targets prediction (EN-10) ranges moves without the mover, so it never predicts a heavy walking through the Cyclone, while the real turn does. *Built* (no source)
- **EN-19. Firing spots.** A ranged enemy doesn't pick a shot whose line of fire is blocked ([Combat rules](combat-rules.md) CR-57). It also drops a move destination from which every enemy in its shot's reach is behind a blocker, unless it has something else to do there (a melee attack in reach, a heal or summon with a valid target). The Dark Shaman is support first: any ally in its heal range, hurt or not, keeps the spot even when the Cyclone blocks its bolt (D-543); its summon, aimed at the area around it, also keeps every spot while it is off cooldown. Destinations with a clear shot, or with no enemy in reach (closing in), are kept. It isn't forced to move: an Archer already at a blocked spot may stay, and a blocked Archer may back off rather than take a clear cell. *Built*
- **EN-10.** The player can see who each enemy plans to hit next, and hovering an enemy shows its threat. New enemy types are introduced with a short close-up the first time they appear. *Built* (no introduction text yet: the narrative agent drafts it, THJ approves, D-195). Enemy abilities with copied titles and text (the Queen's charge and the Deev's pools titled "Shield Dash" with John's description, the Deev's wave "Taunt") get their own, now (D-195)

## Alert state (Unaware and Alerted)

Enemies wait until they notice the family. Each one is **Unaware** or **Alerted** (working names, pending the narrative agent and THJ): an Unaware enemy holds its tile and does nothing until a family member comes within its sight, something happens to it or near it, or a neighbour wakes. This gives every fight a "before": the family chooses where, when and how to start it. On by default in every level, with no global switch; levels and units opt out (EN-17). Design and reasoning: [`plans/2026-10-23-enemy-alert-state-design.md`](../../plans/2026-10-23-enemy-alert-state-design.md), with THJ's answers D-154 to D-167 on top (where they differ, the decisions win).

- **EN-12. Unaware and Alerted.** At level start, every unit of a faction hostile to the player is **Unaware**, unless it is exempt (EN-17) or a unit hostile to it is already within its sight (EN-13), in which case it starts **Alerted**. An Unaware enemy takes no action on its turn: no move, attack, heal or summon. It is skipped like a Stunned one (CR-2) and is left out of the next-targets list (EN-10). An enemy never goes back to Unaware. Units that appear mid-fight follow the same rule: Shaman summons, corruption-pool and wave spawns and Worm split children are Unaware when they appear, or Alerted at once if a hostile unit is already in their sight (D-156). A revived unit keeps the state it had. An enemy turn in which every enemy is Unaware still shows its turn banner, then ends at once. *Built* (D-154, D-156)
- **EN-13. Sight.** An Unaware enemy becomes Alerted when a unit hostile to it **stops** on a tile within its sight. That unit can be a Bergson, the allied Toba, the Deer, a protectee such as the Whitemother stag, or a decoy. Sight is counted in Manhattan steps (four directions, CR-15) from the nearest tile of the enemy's footprint to the nearest tile of the other unit's, through walls, gaps and units: there is no line of sight. "Stops" means the end of a walk, a push or pull, a Command move, a swap, a teleport (Blink), a jump (Vault), a Duo move (the Dance, the Footrace) or appearing on the board (deploy, decoys). Passing through sight in the middle of a walk doesn't count. A full sight check also runs after every action and, as a safety net, at the start of each enemy turn. The ranges are unit attributes with a per-level bonus (table below; D-164, D-165). *Built*
- **EN-14. Noise.** An Unaware enemy becomes Alerted when (a) something really happens to it, from any side: an ability's effect lands on it (damage, a status, a push, Command, a heal, so an awake Shaman's heal wakes the ally it heals), it dodges a hit, it gains Taunted, or it loses HP from anything (spikes, Burn and other damage ticks); or (b) an ability used by the player's side (the family and its allies, the allied Toba included) affects a tile within the enemy's **alert range** while that ability runs: the tiles it hits or lands on, not its flight path. A tile that was targeted but where the ability applied nothing to the enemy standing there doesn't wake it through (a) (D-161). Moves make no noise: they wake only through sight. Enemy abilities, status ticks and hazards make no noise around them (a hazard a family ability left behind wakes only the enemy it damages). The ability that spawns a unit never wakes that unit (D-161). *Built*. Any Cyclone hit wakes through (a), since the enemy loses HP. A Cyclone pull also wakes the pulled enemy as the move starts, with or without a hit, and a knocked enemy wakes when it lands; the wake spreads as any wake does (EN-15), so one pulled or knocked member can wake a pack (D-542; [Lucy](characters/lucy.md) LU-5, LU-6)
- **EN-15. Spread.** When an enemy becomes Alerted, every Unaware enemy within *its* alert range (Manhattan, footprint to footprint) becomes Alerted too, and those spread it in turn. Spread happens only at the moment of waking: an Alerted enemy walking past a sleeping one doesn't wake it. An enemy that starts Alerted because it sees a hostile unit (at level start or when it appears) spreads the same way (D-160); one that starts Alerted only because of a flag (EN-17) doesn't spread. The Black Claw's Pact Leader call (EN-1) wakes the units it calls, because it taunts them and gaining Taunted wakes (EN-14a): the Dark Claws within 5 and the Black Claws within 10 of the attacked Black Claw (counted in eight directions), even outside the alert range. The call answers any ability used on the Black Claw, an awake Shaman's heal included, so that heal also wakes the pack. *Built* (D-155, D-160)
- **EN-16. Timing.** An enemy woken during the family's turn acts normally on the next enemy turn. An enemy woken during an enemy turn (by a Masher counter, a Family Role, a spread or the turn-start sight check) doesn't act in the rest of that turn and acts from the next one: its AP is closed when it wakes. Waking at level start or on arrival has no delay: the enemy is simply Alerted from the start. *Built* (D-156)
- **EN-17. Exceptions and level settings.** The rule covers only factions hostile to the player. The player's faction and its allies (the allied Toba, the Deer) are never Unaware. Always Alerted, wherever they are placed: the Goblin Queen, the Mountain Deev and the duel's enemy Toba (D-163). A level can set **"all enemies start alert"** ([Missions and structure](missions-structure.md) MI-27) and can add a sight bonus and an alert-range bonus to every enemy in it (a whole number, which may be negative; the result never drops below 0). A unit, on its prefab or as a placed instance, can be set **"starts alert"**. There is no global switch (D-154). Dev scenes that don't use the level base prefab (`SampleScene`, `TestLevel`) have no alert tracker, so their enemies are always alert. *Built* (D-154, D-163, D-164)

8. **Q8.** Alert state, the parts not built yet (left for after THJ's play check, D-158): a wake marker on the move ghost (a faint "!" over each Unaware enemy that stopping on the ghost tile would wake), the same marker while picking an ability's target (EN-14b), a wake sound and enemy barks.
