Five Bergsons fight; three more family members live at Home only. Each fighter has a sheet with stats, abilities, passives, skill tree, gear and run upgrades. Names follow the [Glossary](../../../glossary.md).

| Character | In one line | Joins | Sheet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John | The father: sword-and-shield tank who takes hits for the family | Start | [John](john.md) |
| Linda | Archer: piercing ranged damage, watches her lanes | Start | [Linda](linda.md) |
| Lucy | Fire mage and skirmisher: embers, Cyclone, Blink, follows up every attack | Start | [Lucy](lucy.md) |
| Mark | Monk: chains kills, builds Focus, commands allies, strikes back | After M3 "No More Waiting" | [Mark](mark.md) |
| Toba | Healer and newcomer: heals, shields, swaps, taunts | After M4 "Into the Rootwell" | [Toba](toba.md) |

**At Home only (`thj/home-test`):** Mary (cooks in the Kitchen), Uncle Ben (runs the Workshop) and Kevin (chore seats; no Bond, never on a sortie). Their roles are in the [Demo Spec](../../demo-spec.md).

John, Linda, Lucy and Mark also carry **Cleanse**, a shared ability (its sounds are named Dedication): 50 × damage to Corruption planted within 1 cell, 1 AP, cooldown 1. It doesn't hurt units. Toba doesn't have it. Source: `Features/Player/Shared/Abilities/Cleanse/CleanseAbility.asset`.

Every sheet uses the same sections: at a glance, abilities, passives, skill tree, Revenge, gear, run upgrades, Home, open questions. Shared rules live elsewhere: [Combat rules](../combat-rules.md), [Family systems](../family-systems.md), [Progression and economy](../progression-economy.md).
