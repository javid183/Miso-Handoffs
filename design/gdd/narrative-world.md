# Narrative and world

Owner: narrative agent (THJ approves) · Scope: `all` · Checked against assets: `main` @ `92853a5a9`, 2026-10-16 (mission briefings; no dialogue or sequence assets read) · Mirror: Claude Doc "Miso — Game Design", tab Narrative and world · Up: [Overview](overview.md)

**Everything on this page is Proposed.** It collects the story and world as the game data and the docs use them today. Nothing here is confirmed Children of Morta canon beyond the names ([Glossary](../../glossary.md)); canon is filled in with THJ, and until then this page proposes rather than asserts.

## Premise and tone

- **NA-1.** The Bergson family fights the Corruption together. The story is a family under pressure: warmth against dread ([`vision.md`](../../vision.md), pillar 3). *Planned*
- **NA-2.** Miso is a Children of Morta IP collaboration. Its use of the world and characters needs licensing alignment with Dead Mage and 11 bit studios ([`vision.md`](../../vision.md)). *Open*
- **NA-3.** Each chapter's story sets a main dungeon and a deadline (the Great Moon); side missions carry personal threads ([Missions and structure](missions-structure.md) MI-4). *Planned*
