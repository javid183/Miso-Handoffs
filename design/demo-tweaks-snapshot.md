## 2. How to read this list

**Type**

- **Tech**: code, level or data work.
- **Narrative**: words a person writes (lines, names, descriptions). Goes to the narrative designer.
- **Narrative tech**: we build the container now (dialogue asset, sequence node, bark trigger, text field) with a placeholder good enough to prove the idea; the narrative designer replaces the words. Placeholders are tagged \[PLACEHOLDER\].
- **Art**: sprites, portraits, icons, fonts, animations, level dressing.
- **Design**: needs a layout or encounter pass before it is built.

**Can it be done?** ✓ with what is built · ~ a small new piece · ! new tech: flagged, needs THJ's OK before it starts.

**Owner**: Khash (already assigned), Narrative, Claude (the implementer agents), Art, THJ.

## 4. The Hearth ending (back at Home)

Proposed order (Q9):

1.  The last memory ends and the game cuts to Home. Lucy's lines move out of the level's outro into the Home scene.
2.  At the end of the telling the family notices Ryker isn't there. They can't find her but aren't sure she's lost. Everyone stands up.
3.  Lucy leaves the house, animated, to look for Ryker.
4.  Fade out, fade in, a card: "The day after". In the morning they start to worry ("The door's open. Ryker's gone." stays here).
